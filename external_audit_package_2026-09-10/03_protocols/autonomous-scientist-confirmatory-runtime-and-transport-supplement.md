# Confirmatory Runtime, Transport, and Replication Supplement

Status: prospective pre-freeze supplement. At commitment time, no confirmatory
seed, authority, trajectory, or outcome exists. This supplement operationalizes
requirements already present in the adaptive causal-recovery protocol; it does
not change the primary outcome, contrasts, treatment, model, or scientific gates.

## Primary study

The primary study remains the exact 180-task crossed adaptive causal-recovery
panel: five scientific states by three variable-count tiers by six fault classes
by two replicates. Each task is a matched, randomized three-arm block. The model
is `gpt-5.6-sol` at low reasoning effort, with the prospectively amended ceiling
of 128,000 exact input-plus-output tokens and 900 seconds per arm. Failed,
missing, interrupted, over-budget, and invalid arms score zero. All three terminal
records must be immutable before the authority is opened for scoring. A block
opened but not fully scored after interruption is closed as three failures; no
selective arm rerun is allowed.

## Mechanistic study

One replicate from every state-by-tier-by-fault cell is selected by cell identity,
not outcome, producing 90 tasks. Five Scientist-only removals are run in randomized
order: causal memory, plan admissibility, candidate validation, typed recovery,
and independent promotion. All five terminal records in a task are immutable
before scoring. A component is supported only when its removal both reduces valid
root advance and increases its predeclared targeted failure.

## Cross-domain transport

CausaLab-compatible linear structural causal models remain a positive-control
transport domain, not a second efficacy assay. An independent post-audit provider
creates 30 new tasks from sequestered high-entropy seeds: three tiers by node
counts three through seven by two replicates. The same three arms, model, public
method information, intervention surface, strict hidden scorer, and matched-block
withholding rules apply. The historical 64,000-token CausaLab ceiling is retained
because excluded development runs established saturation within that ceiling.

Transport passes only if the Scientist valid-resolution rate is at least 80%, its
paired point difference is non-negative against both controls overall and within
each tier, provenance/accounting is exact, and there is no human intervention or
selective rerun. These are positive-control and non-degradation criteria; no
CausaLab superiority claim is licensed.

## Independent replication

An institutionally independent laboratory provisions a second, disjoint full
180-task adaptive panel from new entropy and executes the unchanged frozen
pipeline without author involvement in execution or scoring. Its provider, public
bundle, private bundle, run, and signed execution attestation must be distinct
from the primary study. Replication requires simultaneous paired intervals above
zero against both controls, no negative state or tier stratum, false-promotion,
zero-human, provenance/accounting, and typed-recovery integrity gates. The primary
15-percentage-point point-estimate threshold is not imposed a second time; the
replication tests direction and integrity rather than exact effect-size equality.

## Claim integration

No individual analysis may authorize the central claim. A content-addressed
integrator may authorize the bounded Nature-level sentence only when all of the
following are true under one frozen implementation:

1. every primary controlled gate passes;
2. all five mechanistic removals support their components;
3. CausaLab transport passes;
4. the independent replication result and signed execution attestation pass; and
5. provider, bundle, run, analysis, source, and freeze identities reconcile.

Failure of any conjunct narrows the paper automatically. Primary advantage without
mechanistic support is an empirical systems result. Advantage without transport is
benchmark-specific. Reliability improvement without efficacy superiority is a
governance result. Failure to replicate prohibits the central general claim.

## Authorization boundary

This supplement authorizes implementation, qualification, freezing, and external
audit preparation only. It authorizes no provider execution, confirmatory state,
scientific claim, manuscript result, or Nature claim. Provider execution begins
only after a content-addressed freeze and a genuinely independent signed audit
release whose checks are all positive.
