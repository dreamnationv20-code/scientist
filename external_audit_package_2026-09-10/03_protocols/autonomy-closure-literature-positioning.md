# Autonomy Closure: Literature Positioning and Nature Claim Boundary

Status: pre-confirmatory positioning memo, frozen on 8 September 2026. This
memo records the prior art that changed the evaluation strategy before any
successor confirmatory outcome was opened.

## Executive finding

An Article cannot now be built around the claim that an AI system can traverse
the research lifecycle, generate hypotheses, run computational experiments, or
write a paper. Those achievements are already established. The defensible open
question is narrower and more consequential:

> Holding the foundation model, public information, tools, and resource budget
> fixed, does separating generative research intelligence from scientific
> authority cause an autonomous system to make more valid scientific advances?

The intended contribution is therefore not another AI-scientist workflow and
not another causal-discovery benchmark. It is a prospective causal test of an
architecture: unified evidential state, typed proposal interfaces, independent
validation and promotion authority, class-specific recovery, and durable stop
rules.

## Closest prior art

| Work | What is already established | Boundary left open for this programme |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [The AI Scientist](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10265-5) (Nature, 2026) | End-to-end idea generation, code, experiments, analysis, manuscript writing, and review; both template-based and template-free operation. | It does not isolate the causal effect of authority separation against the identical model and budget, with objective hidden scientific truth and false-promotion gates. |
| [Co-Scientist](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10644-y) (Nature, 2026) | Multi-agent hypothesis generation, critique, ranking, evolutionary tournaments, persistent context, and biomedical validation. | It is designed for a scientist-in-the-loop setting and does not test a closed execution authority against a matched monolithic agent on unseen end-to-end episodes. |
| [POPPER](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/huang25n.html) (ICML, 2025) | Automated hypothesis validation through sequential falsification with statistical error control. | It validates supplied hypotheses; it does not test autonomy closure across mission choice, diagnosis, invention, experiment design, execution, failure recovery, and programme stopping. |
| [ScienceAgentBench](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/f12b4df26344f3be803c06b555252efe-Abstract-Conference.html) (ICLR, 2025) | 102 expert-validated, data-driven scientific coding tasks from 44 papers, with low end-to-end solve rates for current agents. | It primarily measures task execution against published targets, rather than the validity of prospectively chosen scientific conclusions. |
| [PaperBench](https://openai.com/index/paperbench/) and [MLE-bench](https://openai.com/index/mle-bench/) | Objective environments for research replication and machine-learning engineering. | They test demanding research execution but do not by themselves identify why scientific state, verification, and stopping fail to compose. |

| [AgentFixer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.29848) and [HarnessFix](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06324) | Trace-guided detection, classification, and repair of agent or harness failures. | Their target is general agent reliability, not independent scientific evidence authority, false promotion, and research-program termination. |
| [Petri-bench](https://petri-labs.org/bench/) (2026 working draft) | Procedurally generated causal worlds over five validated simulations; blind budgeted experimentation; objective correctness, method, and efficiency scoring; process-integrity audits; frontier-model and algorithmic baselines. | It exposes the scientific-method failure surface but does not test whether a general authority-separated research architecture repairs it under a matched model and budget. |

## Decisive overlap with Petri-bench

Petri-bench makes the originally planned single-grammar generated-world study
scientifically redundant as a headline contribution. Its five simulation
families, three difficulty tiers, fresh seeded truths, objective scorer, and
process audit are broader than the current in-house causal-state-machine
grammar. Its reported GPT-5.6-Codex trajectories also identify a particularly
diagnostic target: the model can locate a candidate driver yet omit the
measurement needed for direction, contradict its own measurements, over-test,
or fail to run the factorial required for interactions.

Those are composition failures, not merely knowledge failures. They motivate a
matched intervention in which Codex remains the adaptive proposer while a
model-independent authority controls what enters scientific state, which
experiments are admissible, and which claims may be promoted.

The public `petri-labs-mcp` package is MIT-licensed and provides deterministic
simulation and experiment tools. The public episode archive is suitable only
for development because it contains task truths. A confirmatory Petri panel is
eligible only if fresh instances and scoring authorities are generated after
our architecture and analysis are frozen, remain inaccessible to both arms,
and come from an independently audited generator or the benchmark maintainers.

## Novelty claim to test

The causal treatment is **scientific authority separation**, not extra model
intelligence. The full treatment comprises:

1. one event-sourced authoritative state linking every action, model call,
   tool call, artifact, cost, and terminal decision;
2. proposal-only model roles with schemas and bounded re-proposal;
3. deterministic, pre-outcome checks for identifiability, evidence coverage,
   and claim admissibility;
4. validation and promotion actors that are unable to generate the candidate
   they judge;
5. typed failure classes with distinct retry, amend, reframe, and stop actions;
6. semantic memory of predictions, contradictions, exhausted interventions,
   and representation changes; and
7. explicit negative conclusions and durable termination as successful
   outcomes when warranted.

The most important control is not only free-agent Codex. A prompt-equated
monolithic Codex arm must receive the same scientific-method checklist as the
closed system. Otherwise a gain could be attributed to better instructions
rather than to enforcement and authority separation.

## Evidence ladder

### Development only

- Historical takeovers and retained failures test regression and accounting.
- Released Petri episodes test adapter correctness and expose failure modes.
- Synthetic component-pack runs test lifecycle composition.

None can support the central claim.

### Prospective controlled study

Use fresh, answer-blind Petri-style episodes across all five simulations and
three tiers. Compare: (a) free persistent Codex, (b) prompt-equated monolithic
Codex, and (c) the closed Scientist. Model snapshot, reasoning effort, public
packet, tool surface, tool-call ceiling, total model-token ceiling, and wall
time are matched. Objective hidden scoring occurs only after all three terminal
outputs are immutable.

### Failure-mechanism study

Randomly inject infrastructure, implementation, interface, protocol,
measurement, scientific-null, and representation-exhaustion events before
episode generation. This study tests whether the typed recovery policy, rather
than generic retry, explains any long-horizon advantage.

### Transport and discovery study

Run the frozen system and both Codex controls on computational research
episodes outside the causal-world grammar: research engineering or replication,
systems optimization, and small-model mechanism discovery. Report these by
domain; do not silently pool heterogeneous outcomes.

### Independent replication

A separate evaluator must regenerate the strongest panel or score a sealed
rerun without access to our development traces. Replication direction and all
integrity gates are required for the broad Nature claim.

## Falsifiers and downgrade paths

- If prompt-equated Codex matches the closed system, the result supports a
  scientific-method prompting recipe, not authority separation.
- If the closed system reduces false promotion but not valid root advance, the
  result supports reliability or governance, not superior autonomous science.
- If it wins only on Petri-style worlds, the result is benchmark-specific
  procedural scaffolding.
- If it wins by using curated intervention values, hidden evaluator knowledge,
  more model tokens, more tools, or post-outcome repair, the comparison is
  invalid.
- If a component ablation does not remove or attenuate the effect, the proposed
  mechanism is unsupported even if the full system scores well.
- If independent replication fails, a general Nature Article claim is not
  authorized.

## Immediate decision

Do not implement the two remaining home-grown generated grammars as the next
headline milestone. First qualify an external Petri adapter on released
development episodes, freeze the three-arm protocol and contamination boundary,
and obtain a source of fresh independently held task authorities. In parallel,
select a tractable external research-execution panel for cross-grammar
transport. Confirmatory execution remains blocked until those dependencies and
an independent pre-execution audit pass.
