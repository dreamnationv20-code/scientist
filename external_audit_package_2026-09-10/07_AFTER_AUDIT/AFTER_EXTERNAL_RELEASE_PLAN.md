# Plan after the external audit release

This sequence is mandatory. Later stages cannot rescue failure at an earlier
central gate.

## 1. Validate and register the release

- Verify the returned archive digest, freeze identity, signed attestation and all
  required Boolean attestations.
- Reject releases with missing findings, unresolved exceptions or identity
  mismatch.
- Store the release and audit log immutably; no frozen implementation byte may be
  edited afterward.

If this fails: address the audit finding, make a new identified freeze, and repeat
independent audit. Do not provision confirmatory tasks against the rejected
freeze.

## 2. Provision the primary panel

- The independent provider generates fresh entropy and an exact 180-task public
  bundle plus a private authority bundle.
- Publish task, seed-policy, generator and authority-commitment hashes while the
  private authority remains sequestered.
- Verify zero overlap with every development task identity.

If this fails: discard the unexecuted bundle, record the provisioning failure and
reprovision under the same frozen rules. If secrecy was breached, never use that
bundle for confirmation.

## 3. Execute and analyze the primary matched study

- Run 180 matched tasks in three arms: authority-separated Scientist,
  prompt-equated monolithic model and free monolithic model.
- Use the same `gpt-5.6-sol` model, low reasoning effort, public packet, tools,
  128,000-token ceiling, 900-second ceiling and tiered measurement budget.
- Commit all 540 terminal records before opening private authority and scoring.
- Apply the frozen paired analysis and every conjunctive success/safety gate.

If the primary gates fail: stop the confirmatory programme and write the bounded
null/diagnostic paper. Do not spend on ablations or replication and do not claim
an autonomy advantage.

## 4. Identify mechanism and test transport

Only after primary success:

- execute the 90-task, five-removal mechanistic panel (450 episodes); and
- execute the 30-task CausaLab transport panel (90 episodes).

If a removal is unsupported: narrow the mechanism claim to the supported
components. If transport fails: limit the claim to the primary task family. A
mechanism or transport failure cannot be hidden by the primary average.

## 5. Freeze and run the end to end closure study

After the frozen authority study establishes its bounded claim, finalize the
separate protocol in
`03_protocols/autonomy-closure-end-to-end-evaluation-protocol.md`:

- fix the unseen root-objective panel, domain strata, power analysis and exact
  statistical procedure;
- bind the complete goal graph, discovery, memory, recovery and claim components
  under a new content identity;
- obtain a separate audit of intervention accounting, information separation and
  resource matching; and
- compare the complete Scientist with prompt-equated and free standalone agents
  under identical models, tools and budgets.

If at least 90% of complete-system episodes are not intervention-free, the system
has not established autonomy closure. If closure succeeds without a valid-root-
advance advantage, report operational autonomy without scientific superiority.
Neither result may be hidden by the earlier authority study.

## 6. Run the separate independent replication

Only after primary, ablation and transport success, the second external party
provisions and executes a disjoint 180-task replication (540 episodes). The
authors receive the identified evidence package and signed attestation.

If replication fails: do not submit the broad central claim. Report the original
effect and failed replication transparently, investigate heterogeneity without
retrofitting the frozen primary result, and design a new prospective study.

For the broad autonomy-closure claim, replication must include disjoint unseen
root objectives and reproduce both the intervention and scientific-validity
gates. Replication of the frozen authority study alone supports only its narrower
causal claim.

## 7. Integrate evidence and lock the claim

- Run the deterministic evidence integrator over the identified primary,
  ablation, transport, end-to-end closure and replication artifacts.
- Permit positive manuscript language only if it returns
  `nature_central_claim_authorized=true`.
- Run the fail-closed publication compiler to recompute all reported analyses and
  populate the Article template.

If integration fails: follow its declared downgrade path. No human or model may
override the Boolean authorization.

## 8. Add ecological evidence and prepare submission

- In parallel with the independent replication, freeze at least nine
  naturalistic matched research campaigns with independent curators and blinded
  validators.
- Deposit code, public tasks, receipts, outcomes and analysis manifests in a
  persistent repository with DOIs, subject to legitimate secret-redaction needs.
- Produce final result figures, Extended Data, reporting summaries, author
  contributions, competing-interests statement and AI-use disclosure.
- Send the prepared presubmission enquiry only after the causal claim is
  authorized and the naturalistic evidence is credible.

If the naturalistic extension is weak but the controlled programme passes:
target a rigorous specialist venue first or submit a narrower methods paper. Do
not present synthetic-task success as universal autonomous science.
